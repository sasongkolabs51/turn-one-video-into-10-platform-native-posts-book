### 10.4.3 Pre-Deepening Quality Check

Use this checklist after generating your Link 2 draft to verify it is structurally sound and voice-consistent before proceeding to editorial deepening.

- [ ] The draft follows the approved outline's structural arc.
- [ ] Voice consistency scores at least four out of five on your Voice Accuracy rubric.
- [ ] Placeholder anecdotes are clearly marked and easy to locate for replacement.
- [ ] A contrarian insight or unexpected perspective appears in the designated section.
- [ ] No paragraphs feel generic, corporate, or detached in tone.

If you can't tick every box:
- If the draft suffers from Voice Drift, do not proceed to Link 3; instead, regenerate Link 2 with stricter voice constraints to avoid compounding the scaling trap.

### 10.4.4 The Integration Quality Check

Use this checklist after the AI integrates your "yes, and" offer to ensure your personal perspective was woven in naturally rather than just appended.

- [ ] My personal perspective is integrated into the paragraph's flow, not just tacked onto the end.
- [ ] The paragraph still sounds exactly like me, avoiding any committee-written tone.
- [ ] If my thought challenged the original argument, the intellectual tension remains visible and unresolved.
- [ ] The paragraph is demonstrably stronger and deeper than the original AI draft.
- [ ] I would be comfortable publishing this exact paragraph under my name without further edits.

If you can't tick every box:
- If the AI smoothed over your contrarian thought, provide a second, highly specific offer that forces the AI to maintain the tension rather than resolving it.

### 10.4.5 Voice Drift Audit

Use this quality assurance checklist when reading a batch of generated pieces in sequence to catch subtle tonal shifts before scheduling.

- [ ] Read all generated pieces in sequence without making any edits.
- [ ] Hook architecture check: Every opening follows your documented pattern without defaulting to generic openers.
- [ ] Lexical consistency check: Signature phrases and contractions remain present throughout, even in longer outputs.
- [ ] Emotional register check: The tone matches your Voice Fingerprint without shifting toward preachy or clinical.
- [ ] Identify the specific piece where drift first appeared and the exact prompt that generated it.

If you can't tick every box:
- If you detect lexical drift in longer essays, add a reinforcing constraint to that specific prompt to increase the frequency of contractions and direct reader address.

### 10.4.6 Full Chain Completion

Use this final checklist at the end of every amplification chain to ensure no critical editorial steps were skipped.

- [ ] Link 1 outline was explicitly approved and archived in the Hub.
- [ ] Link 2 draft was generated with all voice constraints strictly applied.
- [ ] Placeholder anecdotes were identified and replaced with real, lived stories.
- [ ] Link 3 deepening rounds were completed on at least three key paragraphs.
- [ ] Final piece scored four or above on both Voice Accuracy and Emotional Resonance.
- [ ] Completed piece was saved to the Hub's final spoke section.

If you can't tick every box:
- If you skipped the deepening rounds to save time, recognize that you are falling into the scaling trap and prioritize editorial depth over raw output volume next time.
