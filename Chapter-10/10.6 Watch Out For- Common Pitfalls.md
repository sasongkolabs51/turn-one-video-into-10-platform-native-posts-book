# 10.6 Watch Out For: Common Pitfalls

- Voice Drift occurs when the AI gradually loses the sharpness of your hook style or the informality of your lexicon, especially in longer outputs or when chaining many prompts.
- The Scaling Trap happens when you produce multipiece content without a quality assurance system, resulting in published pieces that feel slightly off and subtly disconnect your audience.
- AI Voice Mistrust Paralysis is the lingering fear that scaling content will inevitably erode authenticity, which is neutralized by treating the AI as an improv partner rather than a mere drafter.
