### 8.3.7 Monthly Exemplar Loop Ritual

Use this checklist during your monthly review to ensure your prompt library evolves alongside your audience's preferences.

- [ ] Identify top three posts by engagement rate across all platforms from the past month.
- [ ] Write a one-line diagnostic for each post explaining why it worked.
- [ ] Update prompt templates if a pattern emerges across two or more top performers.
- [ ] Archive the oldest Reference Example in your prompt bank.
- [ ] Replace it with the top performer from this month.
- [ ] Run a test generation with the new Reference Example to confirm voice consistency.
- [ ] Document any prompt changes in a version log for future reference.

If you can't tick every box:
- If you lack three high-performing posts, focus on the single best post and diagnose why it outperformed the rest, avoiding the trap of promoting mediocre content into your Exemplar Loop.
