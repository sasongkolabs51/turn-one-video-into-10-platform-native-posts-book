# 8.5 Watch Out For: Common Pitfalls

- Generic AI Output occurs when prompts rely on safe, familiar hooks and predictable argument structures that get scrolled past in an AI-saturated feed.
- Hook-Context Mismatch happens when an excellent hook architecture is inappropriate for the specific content's emotional tone, leading to high click-through but low engagement.
- Fatigue Pattern is a gradual performance decline that occurs when a previously successful hook pattern becomes stale as audience expectations and algorithms evolve.
- Emotional Flatline occurs when content is structurally perfect but emotionally inert, resulting in decent likes but low share rates because it reads like a lecture.
