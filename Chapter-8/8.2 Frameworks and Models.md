### 8.2.1 Platform Hook Architectures

**How to fill this in:** Use this decision tree to select the most effective hook architecture for your content based on platform and emotional tone.

What is the primary emotional register and platform for your content?

- Analytical Content (Teaching a framework)
  - Twitter: Use the Bold Reframe or Specificity Bomb to challenge a belief or imply a story.
  - LinkedIn: Use the Insight-First Headline to name a professional struggle and imply a solution.
- Personal Content (Story of struggle or transformation)
  - LinkedIn: Use the Vulnerability Ledger to establish stakes and credibility through disclosure.
  - TikTok: Use a spoken pattern interrupt paired with a visual metaphor.
- Contrarian Content (Challenging conventional wisdom)
  - Twitter: Use the Contrarian Cliffhanger to state a controversial position and stop.
  - TikTok: Use the Spoken Contradiction to break the hypnotic scroll immediately.
