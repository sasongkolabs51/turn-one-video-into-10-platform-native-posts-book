# 8.12 About This Workbook

The frameworks, terminology, and exercises in this workbook are drawn from Turn One Video Into 10+ Platform-Native Posts for Burned-Out Multiplatform Creators in Under an Hour: A Step-by-Step AI System to Multiply Reach, Preserve Voice, and End Burnout — in Just 60 Minutes by Umberto Castagliano. 

This workbook is intended as a companion. It is not a substitute for the chapter, and it does not reproduce the chapter's text.
