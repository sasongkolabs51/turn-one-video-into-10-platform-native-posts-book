### 12.4.3 Monthly Evolution Ritual

Use this checklist during your ninety-minute monthly session to ensure your system improves by at least one measurable increment.

- [ ] Identify top three performing posts from the past month by engagement rate
- [ ] Add each to the Reference Example Library with a one-line diagnostic
- [ ] Update one prompt template based on a pattern across two or more top performers
- [ ] Archive any Reference Example older than six months
- [ ] Check in with your Transformer Circle and share one prompt update
- [ ] Log the changes in your prompt version log for future reference

If you can't tick every box:
- If you skip this ritual, treating mastery as a finish line rather than a permanent posture leads to system decay and isolated struggle.
