# 12.10 Prompt Library: AI Prompts

### 12.10.1 Manifesto Drafter

**When to use this:** When you need to codify your core editorial philosophy and AI collaboration rules into a single anchoring document.

**Expected output:** A concise, single-page Content Transformer Manifesto tailored to your creative identity.

```text
Act as an editorial coach. I need to draft my Content Transformer Manifesto. Ask me a series of targeted questions to uncover my core editorial philosophy, my non-negotiable weekly rhythms, and my strict rules for AI collaboration. Once I answer, synthesize my responses into a concise, single-page manifesto that anchors my identity as an AI-augmented creator. My initial thoughts are: [INITIAL_THOUGHTS]. Ask clarifying questions before answering if the reader's input is ambiguous. Say "I don't know" rather than invent facts, statistics, or citations.
```

**How to adapt:** Drop your rough ideas about your creative boundaries and goals into [INITIAL_THOUGHTS].
