# 1.9 Prompt Library: AI Prompts

### 1.9.1 Voice Fingerprint Codifier

**When to use this:** Use this when you need to document your unique writing mechanics so an AI can replicate your tone.

**Expected output:** It produces a structured style guide detailing your specific hooks, sentence rhythms, and transition patterns.

```text
Act as an expert editorial analyst. I want to codify my unique writing voice into a teachable system. Here is a sample of my highest-performing content:
[PASTE_CONTENT_SAMPLE]

Analyze this text and extract my "Voice Fingerprint." Break it down into the following mechanical patterns:
1. Opening hooks (how I start)
2. Sentence rhythm and length variations
3. Transition styles between ideas
4. Argument structure and pacing

Provide a concrete rule for each pattern that an AI could follow to mimic this voice. 

Before generating the final guide, ask me clarifying questions if the provided sample is too short or ambiguous to determine a clear pattern. If you cannot determine a specific pattern from the text, say "I don't know" rather than inventing a stylistic quirk. Do not invent facts or statistics about my audience.
```

**How to adapt:** Replace `[PASTE_CONTENT_SAMPLE]` with 300-500 words of your best native writing.

### 1.9.2 Editorial Transformation Analyzer

**When to use this:** Use this when reviewing past content rollouts to determine if you merely distributed copies or architected platform-native experiences.

**Expected output:** It outputs an audit categorizing your past assets and highlighting missed opportunities for structural adaptation.

```text
Act as an Editorial Director. I am going to provide a list of recent content assets I published across different platforms.
[LIST_OF_ASSETS_AND_PLATFORMS]

Analyze each asset and categorize it as either "Mechanical Distribution" (slightly modified copy pushed to a platform) or "Editorial Transformation" (structurally adapted to fit the platform's native experience).

For each item categorized as Mechanical Distribution, suggest one specific way it could have been structurally transformed to better fit that specific platform's audience.

Before answering, ask clarifying questions if the platform context or the nature of the asset is unclear. If you are unfamiliar with the specific platform's native format, say "I don't know" rather than guessing its features. Do not invent engagement statistics.
```

**How to adapt:** Fill in `[LIST_OF_ASSETS_AND_PLATFORMS]` with your last 3-5 pillar content rollouts and where they were posted.

### 1.9.3 Hub-and-Spoke Blueprint

**When to use this:** Use this when preparing to create a new pillar asset and needing to structure the master Hub document before generating platform-specific Spokes.

**Expected output:** It provides a detailed outline for a centralized Hub document containing modular components for future repurposing.

```text
Act as an information architect. I am planning a new pillar content piece about [CORE_TOPIC].

I need to build a centralized "Hub" document using the Hub-and-Spoke mental model. Outline the structure of this master Hub document. It must include modular sections for:
1. The core thesis and primary argument
2. Supporting stories and case studies
3. Anticipated audience objections and rebuttals
4. Actionable steps or frameworks

For each section, provide a prompt I can use later to extract a specific "Spoke" (e.g., a short video script, a newsletter, a carousel) from that exact module.

Ask clarifying questions before answering if my target audience or the specific format of the pillar piece is missing. If you lack context on how a specific module translates to a niche platform, say "I don't know" rather than inventing a non-standard content format.
```

**How to adapt:** Change `[CORE_TOPIC]` to the main subject of your upcoming video, podcast, or long-form article.

### 1.9.4 AI Voice Drift Troubleshooter

**When to use this:** Use this when AI-generated drafts sound generic or fail to capture your specific mechanical patterns and sentence rhythm.

**Expected output:** It delivers a diagnostic breakdown of why the AI missed your voice and specific instructions to correct the prompt.

```text
Act as an AI prompt engineer. I am struggling with AI outputs that sound generic and do not match my personal voice.

Here is my documented Voice Fingerprint (my mechanical patterns):
[YOUR_VOICE_FINGERPRINT]

Here is the prompt I used:
[YOUR_CURRENT_PROMPT]

Here is the generic output the AI produced:
[AI_OUTPUT]

Diagnose why the AI failed to replicate my voice. Point out exactly where my current prompt ignored my Voice Fingerprint. Then, rewrite my prompt to strictly enforce my mechanical patterns, specifically focusing on my opening hooks and sentence rhythm.

Ask clarifying questions before answering if the Voice Fingerprint rules are contradictory or too vague to enforce. If you cannot identify the exact cause of the generic tone, say "I don't know" rather than making up a technical limitation of the AI.
```

**How to adapt:** Insert your codified voice rules into `[YOUR_VOICE_FINGERPRINT]` and paste your current workflow into the other placeholders.

### 1.9.5 Editorial Director Delegation Matrix

**When to use this:** Use this when you want to scale your content operation by delegating structural adaptation to AI or human team members while retaining creative judgment.

**Expected output:** It generates a delegation matrix defining what tasks to hand off and what high-level decisions you must keep.

```text
Act as a creative operations consultant. I am adopting the "Editorial Director" identity for my content business, which focuses on [YOUR_NICHE_OR_INDUSTRY].

I want to delegate the structural adaptation of my content to [AI_TOOLS_OR_HUMAN_TEAM].

Create a delegation matrix with two columns: "Delegate" and "Retain."
Under "Delegate," list the specific mechanical tasks involved in adapting a pillar asset into platform-native spokes (e.g., formatting, extracting quotes, adjusting length).
Under "Retain," list the high-level creative judgments I must keep as the Editorial Director (e.g., core thesis approval, final narrative arc).

Ask clarifying questions before answering if my current team size or technical stack is unclear. If you are unfamiliar with the standard workflows in my specific industry, say "I don't know" rather than inventing industry-specific jargon.
```

**How to adapt:** Update `[YOUR_NICHE_OR_INDUSTRY]` and `[AI_TOOLS_OR_HUMAN_TEAM]` to match your actual business context.
