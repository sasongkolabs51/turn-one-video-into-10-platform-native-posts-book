# 1.5 Watch Out For: Common Pitfalls

- Treating repurposing as mere distribution by pushing slightly modified copies of the same content to every platform without structural adaptation.
- Working from scattered artifacts instead of a centralized Hub document, which forces you to repeatedly re-extract insights under time pressure.
- Believing your voice is an uncodifiable mystery, which condemns you to manually rewriting every AI output and traps you in a burnout loop.
