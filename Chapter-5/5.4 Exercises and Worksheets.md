### 5.4.4 Prompt Template Completeness

Use this checklist before you begin testing to verify that every prompt in your library contains all five essential components.

- [ ] **Role:** A clear persona that orients the AI's tone and expertise is defined.
- [ ] **Tone & Voice Constraints:** Your Voice Fingerprint is encoded as specific, actionable rules.
- [ ] **Structural Rules:** Platform-native grammar from your Playbook is explicitly detailed.
- [ ] **Reference Example:** A high-performing post of yours is pasted for stylistic modeling.
- [ ] **Constraint:** Explicit instructions on which Hub blocks to use and what not to invent are included.

If you can't tick every box:
- Generic opening creep occurs when the AI defaults to bland statements because your Voice Fingerprint block lacks strong negative examples.
- Format bleed happens when the AI ignores platform boundaries due to vague Structural Rules.

### 5.4.5 Platform-Format Playbook Integration

Use this checklist to verify that your tested prompt templates are correctly pasted into your Platform-Format Playbook for unified access.

- [ ] Twitter prompt is pasted into the Twitter section of the Platform-Format Playbook.
- [ ] LinkedIn prompt is pasted into the LinkedIn section of the Platform-Format Playbook.
- [ ] Newsletter prompt is pasted into the Newsletter section of the Platform-Format Playbook.
- [ ] Each prompt sits directly below the format rules it enforces.
- [ ] The Platform-Format Playbook is saved in a location you can access during every sprint.

If you can't tick every box:
- Revisit your document structure and ensure your prompts are physically located next to the format rules they enforce to prevent context-switching friction.
