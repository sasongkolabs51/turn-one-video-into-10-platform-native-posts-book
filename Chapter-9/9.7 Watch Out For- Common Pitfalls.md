# 9.7 Watch Out For: Common Pitfalls

- Recency Bias occurs when you over-score recently published pieces on emotional resonance simply because the memory of publishing them is fresh, rather than relying on actual engagement data.
- Metric Myopia is a matrix blind spot where you over-index on a single dimension like shareability because it is easy to measure, while ignoring metrics that correlate with actual business outcomes.
- The No-Data Trap happens when new pillar pieces languish at the bottom of the matrix because they lack historical engagement data, preventing them from ever getting a Discovery Sprint to prove their potential.
