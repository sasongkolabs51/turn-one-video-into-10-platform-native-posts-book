### 9.4.3 Monthly Matrix Maintenance Ritual

Use this checklist during your monthly review to ensure your decision matrix remains a living, accurate tool rather than a static artifact.

- [ ] Update scores for any pillar pieces repurposed since the last review using actual performance data.
- [ ] Re-score older pieces to check for Evergreen drift based on shifting context.
- [ ] Review your Metrics-and-Goals Map and adjust weightings if business goals have changed.
- [ ] Identify one high-scoring piece you have not repurposed yet and schedule it for the upcoming sprint.
- [ ] Document any weight adjustments or scoring changes in a version log.

If you can't tick every box:
- If you lack new performance data, avoid the Recency Bias trap by relying strictly on the original engagement metrics rather than your memory of how the piece felt to publish.
