# 11.7 Watch Out For: Common Pitfalls

- The subtle trap on the other side of competence is that the system still depends on you to push the start button, causing platforms to fall silent when life happens and willpower fades.
- The real danger of a missed sprint is the spiral that follows, where one missed week becomes two and the old guilt-driven scramble returns, leading to system abandonment.
- Automation without accountability leads to drift, causing the system to spin mindlessly into irrelevance without nested feedback loops to catch daily friction and voice shifts.
