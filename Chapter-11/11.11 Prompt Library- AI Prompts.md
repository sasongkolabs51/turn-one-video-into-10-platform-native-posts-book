# 11.11 Prompt Library: AI Prompts

### 11.11.1 Resilience Protocol Activator

**When to use this:** When a life disruption threatens your publishing schedule and you need to instantly select the correct fallback strategy.

**Expected output:** A recommended resilience level (Microsprint, Archive Restore, or Intentional Pause) with an immediate action plan.

```text
Act as a content operations manager. I am facing a disruption to my content schedule. My available time today is [AVAILABLE_TIME], my current energy level is [ENERGY_LEVEL], and the nature of the disruption is [DISRUPTION]. Using the Resilience Protocol Decision Tree, recommend whether I should execute a Level 1 Microsprint, a Level 2 Archive Restore, or a Level 3 Intentional Pause. Provide the exact immediate next step I should take to execute this fallback and maintain momentum. Ask clarifying questions before answering if the reader's input is ambiguous. Say "I don't know" rather than invent facts, statistics, or citations.
```

**How to adapt:** Fill in your current constraints and the specific disruption in the placeholders.

### 11.11.2 Weekly Data Whisper Analyzer

**When to use this:** When you need to compare your Decision Matrix predictions against actual engagement data to refine your scoring model.

**Expected output:** An analysis of prediction deltas and specific recommendations for adjusting your matrix weightings.

```text
Act as a data strategist. I am conducting my Weekly Data Whisper. Here are my original Decision Matrix scores for a recent pillar piece: Evergreen [PREDICTED_EVERGREEN], Resonance [PREDICTED_RESONANCE], Shareability [PREDICTED_SHAREABILITY]. Here is the actual engagement data: [ACTUAL_ENGAGEMENT_DATA]. Analyze the delta between my predictions and the actual results. Identify which dimension I overestimated or underestimated, and recommend specific adjustments to my platform weightings for future scoring. Ask clarifying questions before answering if the reader's input is ambiguous. Say "I don't know" rather than invent facts, statistics, or citations.
```

**How to adapt:** Insert your predicted 1-10 scores and paste your raw analytics export into the placeholders.

### 11.11.3 Sprint Launchpad Architect

**Expected output:** A customized Sprint Launchpad checklist and a set of environmental triggers to automate your start sequence.

```text
Act as a workflow automation specialist. I need to build a Sprint Launchpad that allows me to start my content sprint in under sixty seconds with zero mental friction. My primary tools are [TOOL_STACK] and my committed sprint window is [SPRINT_WINDOW]. Generate a step-by-step Launchpad checklist organizing my links, templates, and Hub access. Then, design three specific environmental or digital triggers I can set up to automatically launch this sequence when my sprint window begins. Ask clarifying questions before answering if the reader's input is ambiguous. Say "I don't know" rather than invent facts, statistics, or citations.
```

**How to adapt:** List your software tools and your recurring sprint day/time in the placeholders.

### 11.11.4 Voice Drift & Defect Diagnoser

**When to use this:** When your daily tune-ups or monthly reviews reveal formatting glitches or subtle voice drift in your AI outputs.

**Expected output:** A diagnosis of the system failure and the exact prompt constraint needed to patch the defect.

```text
Act as an AI systems troubleshooter. My content flywheel is experiencing friction. During my recent review, I noticed the following defect: [DEFECT_DESCRIPTION]. Diagnose whether this is a structural formatting glitch, a broken link issue, or subtle voice drift. Then, write the exact negative constraint or structural rule I need to append to my master prompt template to permanently patch this defect and prevent it from compounding. Ask clarifying questions before answering if the reader's input is ambiguous. Say "I don't know" rather than invent facts, statistics, or citations.
```

**How to adapt:** Describe the specific formatting error or tonal shift you caught in [DEFECT_DESCRIPTION].
