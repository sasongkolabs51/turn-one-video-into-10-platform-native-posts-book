# 7.9 Prompt Library: AI Prompts

### 7.9.1 Scheduler-Ready Prompt Extensions

**When to use this:** When you need to append formatting rules to an existing prompt so the output drops directly into your scheduling tool.

**Expected output:** A block of prompt instructions tailored to a specific scheduler's formatting quirks.

```text
Act as a technical formatting specialist. I need to append a Scheduler-Ready extension to my existing AI prompt so the output imports flawlessly into [SCHEDULING_TOOL]. Generate a strict formatting constraint block I can paste at the end of my prompt. Include rules for line breaks, HTML tags, character limits, and carousel slide separators if applicable. Do not alter my voice or content rules; only add structural formatting commands. Here is my current prompt: [CURRENT_PROMPT]. Ask clarifying questions before answering if the reader's input is ambiguous. Say "I don't know" rather than invent facts, statistics, or citations.
```

**How to adapt:** Replace [SCHEDULING_TOOL] with your platform and paste your draft prompt into [CURRENT_PROMPT].

```text
Act as an editorial operations architect. I need to transform my standard content Hub into a Hub-as-Dashboard structure. Generate a complete markdown template that includes my five core source blocks (Core Thesis, Key Stories, Data Points, Audience Objections, Quotable Lines) followed by dedicated, scheduler-ready output zones for [TARGET_PLATFORMS]. Include status checkboxes and timestamp fields for each output zone so I can track my sprint progress without leaving the document. Ask clarifying questions before answering if the reader's input is ambiguous. Say "I don't know" rather than invent facts, statistics, or citations.
```

**How to adapt:** List the specific platforms you publish to in [TARGET_PLATFORMS].

### 7.9.3 Tool Stack Friction Analyzer

```text
Act as a workflow efficiency analyst. I will describe my current content repurposing tool stack and process. Analyze it to identify round-trip handoffs and format-translation taxes. For each step, estimate the hidden time cost and assign a friction score from 1 to 5. Highlight the exact bottlenecks that should be eliminated or automated to create a unified pipeline. Here is my current workflow: [CURRENT_WORKFLOW]. Ask clarifying questions before answering if the reader's input is ambiguous. Say "I don't know" rather than invent facts, statistics, or citations.
```

**How to adapt:** Detail your step-by-step process and the apps you use in [CURRENT_WORKFLOW].

### 7.9.5 Formatting Failure Diagnoser

**When to use this:** When an AI-generated draft breaks or looks wrong after being pasted into your scheduling tool.

**Expected output:** A diagnosis of the formatting break and a specific prompt constraint to fix it.

```text
Act as an API and formatting troubleshooter. My AI-generated draft failed when imported into my scheduling tool. The specific formatting break I am seeing is [FORMATTING_BREAK]. Diagnose why this is happening based on common AI output quirks (like extra blank lines, merged slides, or broken HTML). Provide the exact negative constraint or structural rule I need to add to my prompt to permanently fix this issue. Here is the prompt I used: [USED_PROMPT]. Ask clarifying questions before answering if the reader's input is ambiguous. Say "I don't know" rather than invent facts, statistics, or citations.
```

**How to adapt:** Describe the exact visual or structural error in [FORMATTING_BREAK] and paste your prompt in [USED_PROMPT].

### 7.9.6 Agency Team Handoff Adapter

**When to use this:** When you need to adapt your solo Hub-as-Dashboard workflow for a multi-person agency or team environment.

**Expected output:** A modified Hub structure with dedicated handoff zones, status tags, and team instructions.

```text
Act as an agency workflow director. I want to extend my solo Hub-as-Dashboard structure for a team of [TEAM_SIZE] people, including roles like [TEAM_ROLES]. Modify the Hub-as-Dashboard template to include team-specific handoff zones, approval status tags, and internal comment placeholders. Additionally, generate a text-based description of a Visual Workflow Diagram that maps how the Hub moves between these team members from initial sprint to final scheduling. Ask clarifying questions before answering if the reader's input is ambiguous. Say "I don't know" rather than invent facts, statistics, or citations.
```

**How to adapt:** Specify your team size in [TEAM_SIZE] and list the specific roles in [TEAM_ROLES].
