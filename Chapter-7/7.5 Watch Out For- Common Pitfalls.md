# 7.5 Watch Out For: Common Pitfalls

- Round-trip handoffs occur when content leaves one tool and returns later in a different format, forcing you to hold context in your head and draining mental energy.
- The format-translation tax is the hidden time cost spent manually adjusting AI-generated drafts to fit a scheduling tool's specific import requirements.
- Tool creep happens when a streamlined pipeline slowly accumulates manual workarounds and friction because the creator neglects a regular workflow audit.
