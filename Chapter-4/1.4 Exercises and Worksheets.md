### 1.4.6 The Hub Quick-Access Test

**Purpose:** Verify that your Hub's blocks are cleanly labeled and formatted by testing your ability to locate specific components in under ten seconds.

**How to fill this in:** Run this speed test on your completed Hub to ensure it delivers on its promise of instant access for fast assembly.

- [ ] I can locate the Core Thesis within ten seconds.
- [ ] I can find a usable story or anecdote within ten seconds.
- [ ] I can find a supporting statistic or study within ten seconds.
- [ ] I can find an audience objection and response within ten seconds.
- [ ] I can find a quotable line suitable for a hook within ten seconds.

If you can't tick every box:
- Revisit the formatting of the block that took too long to find and add explicit white space between entries.
- Ensure your headings match the block names exactly so your eyes can scan the document instantly.
