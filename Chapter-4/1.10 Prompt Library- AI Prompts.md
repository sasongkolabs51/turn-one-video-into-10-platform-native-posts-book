### 1.10.1 Five-Block Hub Builder

**When to use this:** When you have a raw transcript or brain dump and need to structure it into a modular Hub.

**Expected output:** A cleanly formatted document divided into the five core Hub blocks.

```text
Act as an editorial assistant. I will provide a raw piece of content. Your task is to extract and organize the information into the Five-Block Hub Architecture. The five blocks are: 1. Core Thesis (2-4 declarative sentences), 2. Key Stories (setup, conflict, resolution), 3. Data Points (statistics with attribution), 4. Audience Objections (skeptical arguments and responses), 5. Quotable Lines (punchy, shareable sentences). Here is the raw content: [RAW_CONTENT]. Format the output using clear headings for each block. Ask clarifying questions before answering if the raw content is ambiguous or lacks sufficient material for a block. Say "I don't know" rather than inventing facts, statistics, or citations.
```
