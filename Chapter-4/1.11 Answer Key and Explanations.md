**5. C**

**6. Model Answer:** Run the Hub Quick-Access Test by timing yourself to locate five specific components, like a statistic or hook, in under ten seconds each.
Rationale: Running the Hub Quick-Access Test verifies that your blocks are cleanly labeled and formatted by challenging you to locate components rapidly.

**7. Model Answer:** Summarize the story into a self-contained paragraph that clearly captures the setup, conflict, and resolution without including the tangents.
Rationale: The Key Stories exercise guides you to summarize personal stories into self-contained paragraphs capturing the setup, conflict, and resolution.

**8. Model Answer:** Open your Hub Master Template and populate the five blocks using the transcript to build your Command Center before prompting the AI.
Rationale: Populating the Hub-and-Spoke Command Center ensures the material is immediately structured and ready to feed your AI prompts.

**9. Model Answer:** Writing data points as complete sentences with attribution provides essential evidentiary weight and context, preventing the AI from hallucinating sources or misinterpreting isolated numbers.
Rationale: The Data Points exercise helps you capture statistics as complete, standalone sentences with attribution to provide evidentiary weight for platform-specific assets.

**10. Model Answer:** While it saves time upfront, the AI will likely produce cobbled-together, generic results because it has to parse tangents and unstructured conversation instead of clean, modular blocks.
