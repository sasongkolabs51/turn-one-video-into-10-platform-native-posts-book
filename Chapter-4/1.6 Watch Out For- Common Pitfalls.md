# 1.6 Watch Out For: Common Pitfalls

- Believing that a raw, unedited transcript can serve as an editorial Hub, which leads to massive time costs and cobbled-together results.
- Including tangential rants, unedited conversation, or notes to yourself in the Hub, which degrades the quality of AI output.
- Overcondensing stories in the Hub until they read like bullet points and lose their emotional power and narrative arc.
- Falling into perfection paralysis by spending too much time refining the Core Thesis wording instead of completing the remaining Hub blocks.
