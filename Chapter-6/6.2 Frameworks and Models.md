# 6.2 Frameworks and Models

### 6.2.1 The Sprint Triage Framework

**How to fill this in:** Use this decision matrix to rapidly assess AI-generated drafts and sort them into the correct editorial category.

How ready is this AI-generated draft to publish?

- 90% or higher readiness (Hook strong, structure intact, voice consistent)
  - Action: Approve. Quick read for glaring errors, then move on (5 mins max).
- 75–90% readiness (One structural issue, weak transition, or missing anecdote)
  - Action: Lightly Edit. Sharpen hook, fix one issue, or add one element, then approve (10 mins max).
- Below 75% readiness (Voice off, format wrong, or missed Core Thesis)
  - Action: Flag for Rewrite. Note the specific failure and address after the sprint (2 mins max).
