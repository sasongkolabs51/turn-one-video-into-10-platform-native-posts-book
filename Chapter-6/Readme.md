## 6.0 How to Use This Workbook

This workbook is a companion to Chapter 6 of Turn One Video Into 10+ Platform-Native Posts for Burned-Out Multiplatform Creators in Under an Hour: A Step-by-Step AI System to Multiply Reach, Preserve Voice, and End Burnout — in Just 60 Minutes. It is not a replacement for the chapter. Keep the book open beside you as you work.

Every template in this workbook is fillable. Placeholders in [ALL_CAPS] are fields for you to complete. You can write in this book, or copy the templates into a notebook or document.

The sections follow the order of the chapter, so you can work through them from the beginning, or jump to the tool you need.

Templates marked with a reflection section are best done in one sitting.

Trackers are designed to be revisited over time.

If you are working through this book with a group, the Discuss section is designed for that. If you are working alone, you can skip it or use the prompts as writing exercises.
