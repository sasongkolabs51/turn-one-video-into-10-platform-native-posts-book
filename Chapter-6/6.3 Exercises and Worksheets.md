### 6.3.2 Sprint Preflight Checklist

Use this checklist before every sprint to ensure your environment, Hub, and prompts are ready for immediate execution.

- [ ] Hub is fully populated and AI-ready (clear headings, complete story paragraphs, data points on separate lines).
- [ ] Prompt library is open and each template includes the latest Voice Fingerprint and Platform-Format Playbook rules.
- [ ] AI tool is open in multiple tabs or windows, one per platform, with prompts pasted and Hub blocks ready.
- [ ] Timer is set for sixty minutes and visible from my workspace.
- [ ] All unrelated tabs are closed and notifications are silenced.

If you can't tick every box:
- Ensure all tabs are open to fire prompts in parallel, avoiding the sequential processing trap that drains creative stamina.

### 6.3.3 The Final Polish Pass

Use this checklist after triage to verify that all approved and lightly edited drafts meet platform-specific standards before scheduling.

- [ ] Twitter thread: line breaks are correct, hook is strong, closing tweet is reflective.
- [ ] LinkedIn carousel: slide count is correct, one vulnerability moment is present, visual cues are noted.
- [ ] TikTok script: text overlay timing is specified, pattern interrupts occur every five to seven seconds.
- [ ] Newsletter: opening anecdote is personal, one data point is included, closing call to action is clear.
- [ ] All assets: voice is consistent, typos are fixed, platform-specific elements are present.

If you can't tick every box:
- If an asset needs deep structural fixes, flag it for rewrite instead of falling into perfectionism and polishing it now.
