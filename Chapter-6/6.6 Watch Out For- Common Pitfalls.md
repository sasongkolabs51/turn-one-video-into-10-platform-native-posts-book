# 6.6 Watch Out For: Common Pitfalls

- Prompt Drift: Running a sprint where outputs fall below tested quality because the new Hub content is substantively different from the testing material.
- The Overshoot: Falling into perfectionism despite the triage framework, causing the timer to run out before all platforms are assessed.
- The Abandoned Sprint: Skipping the sprint for multiple weeks due to life interruptions and falling back into the manual repurposing trap.
